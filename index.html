About

I’m intrigued by anything and everything that even remotely concerns the human mind. The more complex an idea is, the greater the interest.

My lifetime goal is to build an Autotelic personality.

I’m curious about too many things to even note. I love to understand people and nature in general. Machine Learning/Artificial Intelligence is my primary line of work. Problem solving is my core skill.

I love to read as much as I can. The intention of my writing is to find conflicts in the content I absorb from all my musings.

Hobbies include Cybersecurity, Astrophysics, Literature etc.

  13. 2022 – now

    Hedge Fund

    Lead Engineer. In the fund's hardest stretches, the only engineer on all of it.

    1. Data
    2. Research
    3. Production
    4. Monitoring

    Systems built

    • A pipeline from raw market data to live decisions, automated end to end: data in, models trained across many GPUs, answers served in real time.
    • A distributed search platform for models and strategies. Hundreds of machines, in any cloud or on someone’s desk, join with a few basic requirements, take a share of the work and leave.
    • The whole stack written as code and packed into containers, so it can be rebuilt from scratch on any cloud.

    Problems solved

    • Too few experiments. The old setup could only try so many ideas. The search platform runs 10,000 times as many trials, so good ideas reach production much sooner.
    • Noisy markets. Most market data is noise, so the models are taught better targets over several rounds and act only when they are confident.
    • Cost. Machines are rented only while there is work, the cheap kind that can be taken back at any moment, so the work carries on when one disappears.
    • Speed. Servers moved close to where the trading happens, so decisions arrive in time.
    • The hard years. In the fund’s toughest stretch, testing ideas fast and at scale is what carried it through to growth.
    • Understanding markets from the data up: regimes, patterns, and what it takes to act on them in real time.
