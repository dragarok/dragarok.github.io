Journey

What I have done so far, year by year.

  1. 2001 – 2010

    Adarsha English Boarding Secondary School

    School, from the first year.

    • Nepal Scouts from 2007, later a patrol leader.
  2. 2011
    • President of the school's Child Club, and of its Red Cross Society (2011 – 2012).
    • District-level speech competition held by Children Nepal: first place.
    • District-level debate competition held by Children Nepal: runner-up.
  3. 2012
    • Debate: first place at the JCI district-level debate.
    • Quizzes: first place at a district-level quiz held by the local peace committee; runner-up at two more; third at the District Education Office quiz.
    • Essays: runner-up in two district-level essay competitions, one for World Population Day and one on child welfare.
    • Finished school and sat the School Leaving Certificate exam.
  4. 2013
    • 6th in the country in the SLC exam.
    • A gold medal from the Government of Nepal for it, and the District Education Office's certificate for the district's highest score, given on National Education Day.
    • The Padmahira Purvakarmachari Sewa Puraskar, and a prize from the Pensioner Teachers' Society.
    • Started +2 Science at GoldenGate International College, Kathmandu.
  5. 2014

    GoldenGate International College

    • First place in a high-school quiz competition in Kathmandu.
    • Executive member of the GoldenGate Science Club.
  6. 2015
    • Semi-finalist on a national quiz show broadcast on national television.
    • First in the country in the Grade 12 board exam (HSEB), science stream.
    • Opportunity Funds fellow with USEF Nepal, a programme for students applying to universities in the US.
    • Started Electronics and Communication Engineering at Pulchowk Campus, Institute of Engineering.
  7. 2016

    Pulchowk Campus

    • Volunteered with USEF Nepal.
    • Inter-departmental quiz competition at Pulchowk.
  8. 2017
    • Joined the Pulchowk Debating Club (2017 – 2020).
  9. 2018
    • Won the thematic hardware competition at LOCUS, the tech festival held at Pulchowk, and showcased a project there.
    • Organised the LOCUS quiz and was its main anchor.
    • The win led to an internship at Otonomis, Pepsicola: research on a self-powered smart dustbin that asks for a pickup when it is full.
  10. 2019
    • Robowarz at LOCUS.
    • The Smart Waiter Robot, which delivers food on its own using SLAM and swarm behaviour. I programmed its Raspberry Pi and ATmega328 for the sensors.
    • Final-year project: a smart dustbin that sorts waste. We built our own dataset and compared several pre-trained deep learning models.
    • Graduated in December.
    • Joined LIS Nepal, Lalitpur, as a software engineer in September.
  11. 2020

    LIS Nepal

    Software engineer, until November.

    • Led the research on target- and aspect-based sentiment analysis of tweets: read the papers, implemented a few from scratch, trained transformer models and extended the Tweepy library for the premium Twitter API.
    • Sales and inventory forecasting for HotTopic, a client of Robling, made to run in parallel on Azure.
    • Built the Robling portal end to end with Django, Wagtail and plain JavaScript, with the data-science pipeline inside it.
    • Checked data with SQL, and worked across teams when it went wrong.
    • Mentor at the Yomari Code Camp at LOCUS.
    • A month at Neuromatch Academy, the computational neuroscience summer school.
    • Wrote the AI notes on this site, on transformers, BERT and chatbots like Meena.

    The final-year paper: Comparative Analysis of Multiple Deep CNN Models for Waste Classification (International Journal of Advanced Engineering, 2020)

    The AI notes

  12. 2021

    Freelance

    • My Hair Scope: Django REST APIs, models and views, and the designer's pages in JavaScript, SASS and jQuery.
    • Upwork: a Q-learning agent that predicts Bitcoin's closing price.
    • PD Smart Education: planned the work and the contract for a mobile and desktop app that plays encrypted videos offline, and saw it through to delivery. Built a Tkinter tool that encrypts and compresses the videos and issues licence keys by class, role and number of uses.
  13. 2022 – now

    A hedge fund

    Lead Engineer.

    1. Data
    2. Research
    3. Production
    4. Monitoring
    • One automated system from raw market data to live decisions: data coming in, models trained on it, and answers served in real time.
    • A distributed search platform: hundreds of machines, in the cloud or on someone's desk, can join, take a share of the work and leave.
    • Infrastructure written as code and packed into containers, so the whole stack can be rebuilt anywhere and run at the lowest cost that works.
    • Servers moved closer to where the trading happens.
    • Learning markets from the data up: regimes, patterns, and what it takes to act on them in real time.
