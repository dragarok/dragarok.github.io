Review

It is such a great book. One that shows true nature of manipulation, propaganda, dictatorship, and lies in its blatant form using animals. The animals are representative. Sheeps being animals who don’t think for themselves, and repeat what others chant to them. The pigs, though being the most grotesque ones, preside over all animals. The dictator is named Napoleon in that spirit. Every idea of freedom starts with truthful principles. But over time, evil ones manipulate the idea to make the fight for freedom the fight for their power. When they gain power, they manipulate information to make it seem like their leader gaining power is them getting freedom. Even when the animals are hungry, they feel like they are fighting for freedom. They are misled by songs, poems, decress, and sheeps. Those who fight for freedom are decreed as terrorists since they threaten dictator’s rule. Today’s society is seen similar in that regards. Mass media manipulation is a common tactic used to hold power over people. We are shown what they want us to show. We are being fed information so vigorously creating an emotional upsurge all the time. We feel like we have no time to truly think and introspect. This is what animal farm tells us about the current state of the world.

Media, Disinformation, and Sensationalization is very much depicted here.
