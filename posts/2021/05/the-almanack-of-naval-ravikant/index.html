The Almanack of Naval Ravikant

  • If you’re not willing to do a wholesale , 24/7, 100 percent swap with who that person is, then there is no point in being jealous.
  • Escape competition through authenticity. Trying to do the same thing just the way someone else did won’t work great for you.
  • Happiness: Being in the moment. The fewer desires you have, the more you can accept the present.
  • Reality is neutral. The world just reflects your own feelings back at you.
  • Life isn’t hard. We make it hard. One of the words that makes it hard is the word “should”.
  • When it comes to medicines for the mind, placebo effect is 100% effective. You want to be positively inclined, not incredulous in belief. If it is fully internalized, you will have a positive mindset.
  • Use your judgment to figure out the kinds of environments you can thrive in, and then create an environment around you so you’re statistically likely to succeed.
  • The real truths are heresies. They cannot be spoken. Only discovered, whispered, and perhaps read.

Action

  • Impatient with actions, patience with results.
  • When you say, “I’m going to do this,” and “I’m going to be that,” you’re really putting it off. You’re giving yourself an out. At least if you’re self-aware, you can think, “‘I say I want to do this, but I don’t really because if I really wanted to do it, I would just do it.”
  • Anything you have to do, just get it done. Life is slipping away.

Desire

  • Desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want.
  • Try to have only one big desire in life at one time which will be the axis of your suffering. This is the area where you have chosen to be unhappy.
  • Don’t have two - you will be distracted.
  • It’s way more important to perfect your desires than to try to do something you don’t 100 percent desire.
  • Every time you desire, ask “Is it so important to me I’ll be unhappy unless this goes my way?”

War and Peace

  • When you start a business, in a way, you’re going to war. When you struggle with your roommates as to who should clean the dishes, you’re going to war.
  • Peace is not a guarantee. It’s always flowing. You want to learn the skill of flowing with life and accepting it in most cases.
  • Successful are those who step out of the game entirely, who don’t even play the game, who rise above it. People with such internal mental and self-control and self-awareness, they need nothing from anybody else. Jerzy Gergorek - I.

Life is short

  • Anything from the past is gone. A lot of unhappiness comes from comparing things from the past to the present.
  • Believing in utter insignificance of the self helps you remove desire for the Universe to conform to your will and desire. Your life is a firefly blink in a night.
  • Understanding the futility of what you’re doing can bring great happiness and peace.
  • You are gone in three generations, and nobody cares. Before you showed up, nobody cared.

Habits

  • You have to go through life replacing your thoughtless habits with good ones.
  • Changing habits:
    1. Pick one thing.
    2. Cultivate a desire.
    3. Visualize it.
    4. Plan a sustainable path.
    5. Identify needs, triggers, and substitutes.
    6. Tell your friends.
    7. Track meticulously.
  • Self-discipline is a bridge to a new self-image.

People you are around

  • Are they upbeat?
  • Are those relationships low-maintenance?
  • Do you admire and respect but not envy them?

Conflict

  • Don’t hang around people who are constantly engaging in conflict.

Seeing the truth

  • No. 1 thing that clouds us is our preconceived notions of the way it should be.
  • Your ego doesn’t want to face the truth. The smaller you make your ego, the less desires you have for the outcome, the easier it will be to see reality.
  • Suffering is the moment when we can no longer deny reality.
  • Try to acknowledge publicly if something isn’t going well. This way you don’t delude yourself from what’s actually going on.

Importance of Foundations

  • Knowing how to be persuasive when speaking is far more important than being an expert digital marketer.

Accepting Pain

  • Write down last time such painful thing happened and how you happened to grow after years.
