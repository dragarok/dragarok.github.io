Role of co2 in our body

  • Exchange process of oxygen and carbon-dioxide in lungs isn’t a big mystery. Alveoli, bronchioles and their role is also pretty evident. Then, what’s missing?

  • Breathing more O2 doesn’t mean your body produces more heat or uses that more O2.
  • By breathing slow, you can let your CO2 levels rise in the body without changing your O2 values.

Role of CO2 in weight loss

  • The CO2 in every exhale has weight, and we exhale more weight than we inhale. We lose weight through exhaled breath.
  • For every 10 pounds of fat lost in our bodies, 8.5 is comes out through lungs. Most of it is CO2 mixed with water vapor. So, lungs regulate weight too.

  • Lungs covered with nerves that extend to para at lower lobes and sympathetic at upper lobes. So, when you need to breath fast, breathes are shorter. They activate sympathetic nervous system.
    • If breath is deep, then that simulates para system.

Left and Right Nostril

  • Right yawns awake to greet the sun and left awakens to the fullness of the moon.
  • Right can cause heating in our body and left can create calmness.
  • Each nostril activates opposite side of the brain.
  • Schizophrenic patients were found to have left-nostril breathing dominance since it activates the creative part a.k.a right hemisphere.

The right nostril is a gas pedal. When you’re inhaling primarily through this channel, circulation speeds up, your body gets hotter, and cortisol levels, blood pressure, and heart rate all increase. This happens because breathing through the right side of the nose activates the sympathetic nervous system, the “fight or flight” mechanism that puts the body in a more elevated state of alertness and readiness.

Inhaling through the left nostril has the opposite effect: it works as a kind of brake system to the right nostril’s accelerator. The left nostril is more deeply connected to the parasympathetic nervous system, the rest-and-relax side that lowers temperature and blood pressure, cools the body, and reduces anxiety

Wim Hof Breathing Technique

  • Lie flat on your back with pillow on your head.
  • Relax shoulders, chest, legs.
  • Loop start.
  • Take 30 very deep, very fast breaths into the pit of stomach and let it back out. Use pursed lips only if you cannot breathe using nose.
    • Movement of each inhalation should look like a wave, filling stomach, and slowly moving up through the lungs.
    • Same for exhale. First empty the stomach, then the chest.
  • At end of 30 breaths, exhale to “natural conclusion”. Hold that quarter of air in the lungs for as long as possible.
  • After limit, take huge inhale and hold for 15 seconds. Move that fresh breath around chest and shoulders, then exhale.
  • Now, heavy breathing again.
  • Loop end.
  • 3 times
