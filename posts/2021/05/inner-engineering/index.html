Layers of Self

  1. Annamayakosha : Food body.
  2. Manomayakosha : Mental Body
  3. Pranamayakosha : Energy Body
  4. Vignanamayakosha : Eheric Body [ Vignana: Extraordinary Knowledge ]
  5. Anandamayakosha: Bliss Body [ Nothing Physical ]

Types of yoga

Yoga is chitta, vritti, and nirodha.

  1. Gnana Yoga: Yoga of Intelligence
  2. Karma Yoga: Yoga of Action
  3. Bhakti Yoga: Yoga of Devotion
  4. Kriya Yoga: Yoga of Transforming Energies

Dimensions of Mind

16 dimensions falling into four categories are as follows:

  1. Buddhi : Discerning or Discriminatory Mind.
  2. Manas : Accumulative Dimension of the Mind [ Memory ].
  3. Chitta : Awareness
  4. Ahankara : From which you derive your sense of identity

Chakras

  1. Muladhara, which is located at the perineum, the space between the anal outlet and the genital organ.
  2. Swadhishthana, which is just above the genital organ.
  3. Manipuraka, which is three fourths of an inch below the navel.
  4. Anahata, which is below the point where the rib cage meets the diaphragm.
  5. Vishuddhi, which is at the pit of the throat.
  6. Agna, which is between the eyebrows.
  7. Sahasrara, also known as the brahmarandra, which is at the fontanel on top of the head.

Samadhis

1.Savikalpa (samadhis with attributes or qualities that are very pleasant, blissful, and ecstatic) 2.Nirvikalpa (samadhis that are beyond pleasant and unpleasant, without attributes or qualities). In the case of nirvikalpa samadhi, there is only a single-pointed contact with the body. The rest of the energy is loose and uninvolved with the physical. These states are maintained for certain periods to help practitioners establish the distinction between themselves and their bodies.
