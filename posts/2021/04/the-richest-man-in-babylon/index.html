The Richest Man in Babylon

  • Financial Tips straight with a story that feels good.

Advice is one thing that is freely given away, but watch that you take only what is worth having.

Advice to improve your finances

  1. Learn to live upon less than you could earn.
  2. Learn to seek advice from those who are competent through their own experiences to give it.
  3. Learn to make gold work for you.

Opportunity is a haughty goddess who wastes no time with those who are unprepared.

Managing your expenses

Budget thy expenses that thou mayest have coins to pay for thy necessities, to pay for thy enjoyments, and to gratify thy worthwhile desires without spending more than 9/10th of thy earnings.

  1. WRITE down in BOLD LETTERS for each thing you desire to spend upon.
  2. SELECT those that are necessary and possible through expenditure of 9/10th of your income.
  3. CROSS the rest and consider them but a part of that great multitude of desires that must go unsatisfied and regret them not.

Saving and Investing

Gold in a purse is gratifying to own and satisfieth a miserly soul but earns nothing.

Misfortune loves a shining mark. Gold in a man’s purse must be guarded with firmness, else it be lost. Thus it is wise that we must secure small amounts and learn to protect them before the Gods entrust us with larger.

Is it wise to be intrigued by larger earnings when thy principal may be lost? I say not.

Before thou entrust it as an investment in any field acquaint thyself with the dangers which may beset it.

Provide in advance for the needs of thy growing age and the protection of thy family.

Desires

Preceding accomplishment must be desire. Thy desires must be strong and definite. General desires are but weak longings.

Desires must be simple and definite. They defeat their own purpose should they be too many, too confusing, or beyond a man’s training to accomplish.

Luck

Good luck, we do find, often follows opportunity but seldom comes otherwise.

I did think at first it was my own poor judgment that did cause me loss of many profitable trades. Later, I did credit it to my stubborn disposition. At last, I did recognize it for what it was — a habit of needless delaying where action was required, action prompt and decisive.

Give them a choice of gold and wisdom - what do they do? Ignore the wisdom and waste the gold. On the morrow, they wail because they have no more gold.

Rules of Money

  1. Money comes gladly and in increasing quantity to any man who will put by not less than 1/10th of his earnings to create an estate for is future and that of his family.
  2. Money labors diligently and with equanimity for the wise owner who finds for it profitable employment.
  3. Money clings to the protection of the cautious owner who invests it under the advice of men wise in it’s handling.
  4. Money slips away from the man who invests it in businesses or purposes with which he is not familiar or which are not approved by those skilled in its keep.
  5. Money flees the man who would force it to impossible earnings or who follows the alluring advice of tricksters and schemers or who trusts it to his own inexperience.

Protection

The walls of Babylon represents man’s need and desire for protection. This desire is inherent in human race.

Being Free

If a man has in himself the soul of a slave will he not become one no matter what his birth, even as water seeks its level?

If a man has within him the soul of a free man, will he not become respected and honored in his own city in spite of his misfortune.

Purposes of Money and Investment Plans

  1. The plan doth provide for my future prosperity.
  2. The plan doth provide that I shall support and clothe my good wife who hath returned to me with loyalty from the house of her father.
  3. The plan doth provide that out of my earnings my debts shall be paid. Therefore each time the moon is full, 2/10th of all I have earned shall be divided honorably and fairly among those who have entrusted me and to who I am indebted.

Some more advice

  • What we call our necessary expenses will always grow to equal our incomes unless we protest to the contrary.

Just as weeds grow in a field wherever the farmer leaves space for their roots, even so freely do desires grow in men whenever there is a possibility of their being gratified.

I, the ass, am like many other simplehearted one who starts to help a friend and ends up by doing his task for him.

If you desire to help thy friend, do so in a way that will not bring thy friend’s burdens upon thyself.

Better a little caution than a great regret.
