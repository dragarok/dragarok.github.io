Smriti Yoga

  • Human Memory and Yoga Tutorials

  • Almonds and Saffron in Milk a. Saffron at night. b. Almonds in the morning.

  • Herb infused Ghee: a. Ghee ingested and/or massaged into the head. b. Herbs:

    1. Brahmi [ Bacopamonerri ]
    2. Jyotishmati [ Celastrus Paniculatus ]
    3. Bojho [ Acorus Calamus ]

    c. Brahmi leaves taken in large quantities will produce dizziness. d. Jyotishmati have to be given more carefully. One grain on the first day, increase it to seven grains on the seventh day, and then reduce it to one grain per day.

  • Mnemonic Exercises a. Count the breaths upto an arbitrary number. b. Next, count by skipping two numbers at a time, both ways. [ For one week ] c. Next, count by skipping four numbers both ways. [ For one week ] d. Now increase the numbers at any stage. e. From 108 to 1 million and eight if you prefer and can handle it. f. Keep increasing complexity and permutations. g. Best is to do these with counting of breaths.

  • Verbal Permutations a. Jataa, Maalaa, Shikhaa, Rekhaa, Dhvaja, Danda, Ghana, Ratha. b. Example: Three words of the Rig-veda ‘agnim ide puro-hitam’. It would be recited in the sequence of: 1-2, 2-1, 1-2; 2-3, 3-2, 2-3; 1-2-3, 3-2-1; 2-1-3; 3-1-2; 2-3-1; 3-2-1; 1-2-3.

  • Deep Sankalpa / Power of Intent : Chit-shakti is told to take a certain direction, to channel thoughts of a particular stream of consciousness. Thus,one may create whatever one wishes.

  • Diet of the book writer: a. Morning: One glass of yoghurt. b. Lunch: Large bowls of vegetable, glass of milk or a little fruit. c. Before Evening Lecture: Two hours of silence, a small bowl of fruit, maybe some nuts. d. After lecture at night: Full meal of daal ( lentils ), vegetables and usually one chapatti.

  • Yoga Nidra Prelims: Can be done sitting on a chair, resting on a sofa or laying down. a. Close your eyes, relax, and be aware of your entire body from head to toe. b. Breathe diaphgramatically as though the breath is flowing from head to toes and toes to head. c. Bring your awareness to ajna chakra [ between the eyebrows ] and exhale and inhale three times. d. Bring your awareness to visuddha chakra [ throat center], visualize an immaculate full moon there and exhale and inhale three times. e. Bring your awareness to anahata chakra [ heart center ], exhale and inhale three times.

    1. Feel as if there is a cave in anahata chakra and breathe as if you are inhaling into the cave and exhaling from your toes.

    f. Breathe with no thought, no mantra - but only the awareness of the breath and prana flow. g. When you feel rested, open your eyes and gently sit up.
