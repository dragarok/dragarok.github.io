How to meditate (Zazen)

  • Sitting in lotus position has nothing magical to it. It’s to make legs feel like one: thus expressing oneness of duality. You don’t know which is left one and which is right one.
  • Relax your shoulders and push up towards the ceiling with the back of your head.
  • To gain strength in your posture:
    • Pull your chin in so that you have strength in your posture; thus preventing you from falling asleep.
    • Press diaphgram down towards lower abdomen.
      • NOTE:: Breating might feel a little difficult at first but it’s okay.
  • Hands in cosmic mudra
    • Left on top of right
    • Middle joints of your middle fingers together.
    • Touch thumbs lightly as if there’s a paper between them thus making an oval.
  • Keep mudra as if you are holding something with great care.
  • Hands held against the body with thumbs at about the height of your navel.
  • Arms free and easy, slightly away from your body as if you held an egg under each arm without breaking it.
