Summary

I feel like he had a list of quotes that truly represent Zen first and then wrote the story to obscure these intentionally. I say that while reading the book I didn’t find it that profound, just talking about his motorcycle maintenance, problems and so on. But now while I am reviewing my highlights, it doesn’t feel that shallow. Zen is vey easy to say profoundly because the obvious nature of it is contradictions and depth. And anything can seem deep if you just use heavy words. The beauty of Zen is in the depth that arises alongside simplicity. There is no enough of Zen, but I feel like I have arrived at that simplicity by stripping away my complexities. I think it can be a good book for Zen beginners but they will need to read twice to see a bit of Zen.
